Import from your Kobo Libra Colour

  1. Plug the Kobo into a computer with the USB cable and tap Connect on the e-reader.
  2. Open the KOBOeReader drive and turn on hidden files: on a Mac press ⌘ ⇧ . in Finder; on Windows choose View → Show → Hidden items.
  3. Open the .kobo folder and pick KoboReader.sqlite below. Want to do it from your phone? Copy that file to your phone or cloud drive first, then pick it here.
  4. Eject the Kobo when you are done. Import again any time: only new highlights are added.

Import from your Xteink X4 Pro

  1. Highlights need the CrossPoint Reader firmware. In a book, select text and save it as a clipping. CrossPoint adds every clipping to My Clippings.txt on the SD card.
  2. Connect the X4 Pro to a computer and turn on USB Drive mode, or take out the microSD card and use a card reader.
  3. Pick My Clippings.txt from the top level of the card below. On your phone, copy the file to the phone or a cloud drive first. A Kindle's My Clippings.txt works too.
  4. Import again any time: only new highlights are added. Deleting a clipping on the X4 Pro doesn't remove it from the file, so delete it here as well.
